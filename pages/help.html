YOUR LITTLE GUIDEBOOK

How to Use

Follow these simple steps to create your own retro photostrip!

From camera to keepsake

  1. 1

    Allow camera access

    Click the start button and allow your browser to use the camera.

  2. 2

    Choose a layout

    Select how many photos you'd like in your strip.

  3. 3

    Select a timer

    Pick a 3, 5, or 10 second timer (or turn it off).

  4. 4

    Pose for the camera

    Take your photos one by one. Your strip fills up as you go.

  5. 5

    Make it yours

    Add your favorite filter, frame, or sticker in the editor.

  6. 6

    Save your photo

    Save it to your device, and find it again in My Flashbacks.

A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

FAQ

Why is my camera not working?

Check that your browser has camera permission and no other app is using it. Camera access requires HTTPS or localhost.

Can I use this on my phone?

Yes! Flashback works in modern mobile browsers. You can also upload an existing photo instead.

How do I save my photos?

Press “Save photo” or “Save photo strip” to download the image. Saved memories also appear in the gallery on this device.

How do I print on A4?

Take at least one photo, check the A4 print preview on the Photobooth page, then choose “Print A4 sheet”. In the print dialog, select A4 portrait paper and use “Actual size” or 100% scale if available.

Are my photos private?

Your photos stay in your browser on this device. They are not uploaded to a server.

What if my photo isn't showing?

Try refreshing the page or choosing the photo again. Your browser may have cleared its local storage.
