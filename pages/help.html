YOUR LITTLE GUIDEBOOK

How to Use

Follow these simple steps to create your own retro photostrip!

From camera to keepsake

  1. 1

    Allow camera access

    Click the start button and allow your browser to use the camera.

  2. 2

    Choose a layout

    Select how many photos you'd like in your strip.

  3. 3

    Select a timer

    Pick a 3, 5, or 10 second timer (or turn it off).

  4. 4

    Pose for the camera

    Take your photos one by one. Each shot gets its own guest code before you save the strip.

  5. 5

    Make it yours

    Choose the filter and frame before taking a photo, or edit and save a finished photo in the editor.

  6. 6

    Share privately

    On the hosted site, note the code shown after each shot and give it to that photo's guest. It expires 24 hours after creation.

A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

FAQ

Why is my camera not working?

Check that your browser has camera permission and no other app is using it. Camera access requires HTTPS or localhost.

Can I use this on my phone?

Yes! Flashback works in modern mobile browsers. You can also upload an existing photo instead.

How do I save my photos?

Save the photo or strip to download it. Photos saved in My Flashbacks are local to the booth device.

How do I print on A4?

Take at least one photo, check the A4 print preview on the Photobooth page, then choose “Print A4 sheet”. The preview includes each photo's private code when sharing is configured.

Are guest photos private?

Guest photos are stored in a private Supabase bucket. Only someone with the generated 24-hour code can open a photo; expired files are cleaned up automatically. Do not share a guest code publicly.

What if my photo isn't showing?

Try refreshing the page or choosing the photo again. Your browser may have cleared its local storage.
