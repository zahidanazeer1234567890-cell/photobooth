About Flashback

THE STORY

What is Flashback?

Flashback is a retro-inspired web photobooth application that lets you capture, edit, and decorate your photos with fun filters, frames, and more. It's made for the little moments that deserve a place in your memory book.

We wanted to bring back the joy of squeezing into a booth with your favorite people, waiting for the strip to print, and keeping it forever.

The best things in life
are the moments we share. ♡
✿   ✦   ♡

Why we created it

We believe memories deserve more than a camera roll. Flashback gives you a playful, creative space to make something you’ll want to keep.

OUR PURPOSE

A little joy, saved forever.

Our purpose is to provide a simple, fun and creative way for people to take and customize their photos. We hope Flashback helps you celebrate the people and moments that make life special.
